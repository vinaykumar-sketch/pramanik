Photo Authenticity Checker

Check whether a photo appears to be AI-generated or digitally tampered with — get a probabilistic read, not a black-box verdict.

Prototype Development

What it does

Submit a photo and receive a Low, Medium, or High probability assessment of whether it's AI-generated or tampered with, along with a clear disclaimer about what that result does and doesn't mean.

Where things stand

This app is currently in early stage development. It is not yet publicly available. We're building it deliberately — with privacy, transparency, and child-safety commitments in place from the start rather than added later.
